Services

Comprehensive Psychiatric Evaluations

A thorough assessment of current concerns, symptoms, psychiatric and medical history, and individual needs to support diagnostic clarity and personalized treatment planning.

Medication Management

Individualized medication evaluation and ongoing monitoring to assess effectiveness, tolerability, and treatment progress, with adjustments made when clinically appropriate.

ADHD Evaluation & Management

Comprehensive evaluation of attention, hyperactivity, impulsivity, and related concerns using clinical assessment and standardized screening tools when appropriate, followed by individualized treatment and ongoing management.

Child & Adolescent Psychiatric Care

Developmentally appropriate psychiatric evaluation and treatment for children and adolescents, with family involvement and collaboration with other members of the care team when appropriate.

Adult Psychiatric Care

Comprehensive evaluation and ongoing treatment for adults experiencing a range of mental health concerns, with care tailored to individual needs, goals, and circumstances.

Supportive Psychotherapy

Brief, focused psychotherapy may be incorporated into psychiatric visits when clinically appropriate to strengthen coping skills, emotional regulation, problem-solving, and overall treatment progress.

Diagnostic Clarification & Second-Opinion Evaluations

Comprehensive reassessment for individuals experiencing complex symptoms, diagnostic uncertainty, or previous treatment challenges.

Psychiatric Follow-Up Care

Ongoing visits to monitor symptoms, functioning, treatment response, medication effectiveness, and progress toward individualized treatment goals.

Telepsychiatry

Secure and convenient virtual psychiatric care for eligible patients when clinically appropriate.

Patient & Family Education

Practical education to help patients and families understand diagnoses, medications, treatment options, potential side effects, and strategies for supporting mental wellness.

Care Coordination

Collaboration with therapists, primary care providers, schools, specialists, and other members of the treatment team when appropriate and authorized.

Laboratory Monitoring

Ordering and monitoring relevant laboratory testing when clinically indicated to support safe and effective psychiatric treatment.

Conditions We Treat

Treatment is individualized based on clinical needs and appropriateness for outpatient care. When a higher level of care or specialized service is needed, appropriate referrals may be recommended.
